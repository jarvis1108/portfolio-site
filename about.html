My Story

Finding the "Why" behind my code

I’m a product designer with 3+ years of experience in enterprise and government systems. Most recently, I designed OASIS and ATLAS at NYC Public Schools — student information and special education platforms serving 1,800 schools and 1.1 million students. I graduated from the M.S. HCI/d program at Indiana University Bloomington in 2023. Previously, I’ve worked with BonCamel, Clearviction, Salesforce, Beamable, and Best Buy.

I started as a Software Engineer during undergrad, but found myself more drawn to the why behind my code than the code itself — why does this flow confuse people? why does this screen feel off? That curiosity led me to UX design, and I’ve been working to bridge the two ever since.

I think of myself as a full-stack designer: I own projects from requirements through research, wireframes, and stakeholder reviews to production-ready specs. I’ve also brought AI into my design process — for synthesis, rapid iteration, and content structuring — which lets me move faster without sacrificing depth. The result is work that’s both well-considered and actually buildable.

My Design Should be

Intuitive, unified, and meaningful

Systematic, clear, and grounded.

I used to define good design by how it felt — intuitive flows, consistent patterns, meaningful details. That’s still the foundation. But designing compliance systems for 1.1M students across complex, multi-user workflows sharpened that instinct into something more specific: design that’s systematic in how it connects, clear in how it communicates, and grounded in what can actually be built and maintained.
